Product Entity Matching

Product entity matching with RoBERTa and comparison features

Compare retailer listings using their text and product fields. The model combines a joint text representation with explicit comparisons of titles, model numbers, brands and prices, and returns a score for each pair.

C+ names the final model in this project: RoBERTa with 42 comparison features, averaged across three training seeds.

01

Task and product inputs

The matching task

The same product can have different titles, model-number spellings or missing fields at different retailers. Finding the corresponding records helps align catalogs and inspect duplicates.

Input and output

Input is a pair of product records with title, brand, description, price, currency, native model number and category. The model returns a score from 0 to 1; a calibrated cutoff turns it into a match or nonmatch prediction.

03

How the model scores a product pair

RoBERTa reads the two listings together to represent their text. A separate 42-value vector records explicit comparisons, such as model-digit differences, missing brands and whether prices share a currency. Brand, model number, category and title enter the text branch; description and price contribute through the comparison features. The two branches are combined to produce a pair score.

Compared with L42, the strongest numerical-feature reference on this pool, C+ reduces false matches from 35 to 11 while missed matches increase from 23 to 25, improving F1. AP is only 0.0381 percentage points above independently trained C0, so this comparison does not establish a substantial ranking gain from the 42 features.

The evaluation is retrospective. The fixed pool’s historical exposure record is incomplete, and performance on an independent new Walmart–Amazon sample has not been tested.
