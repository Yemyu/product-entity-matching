Fixed-pool comparison

Retrospective results on 555 product pairs

Compare six methods on the same product pairs: four models based on numerical comparisons, the final text-and-feature model C+, and the independently trained C0 control whose comparison features are set to zero. AP measures ranking; F1 measures decisions at each method’s calibration cutoff.

C+ names the final model in this project: RoBERTa with 42 comparison features, averaged across three training seeds.

01

AP ranking and F1 at calibrated cutoffs

All six methods scored the same 555-pair retrospective pool, including 190 matches. Each method uses its own calibration cutoff for F1. AP compares ranking without that cutoff. Both plot axes cover 0–1, and the table keeps six decimal places.

B22 fits logistic regression on the first 22 comparisons. L30 uses gradient-boosted trees on 30 comparisons; S33 adds three fixed MiniLM semantic/missingness values to that representation. L42 uses all 42 comparisons without RoBERTa, so it supplies the strongest tabular reference measured on this pool. C+ adds paired-text encoding and averages three trained seeds. C0 is trained independently of C+, retains the neural dimensions, and zeros all 42 feature values throughout training and inference. It is evaluated at epoch 4 after four training epochs with the six-epoch scheduler. All six rows use the same evaluation pool; F1 is computed at each method’s calibration cutoff. The comparison is retrospective and does not establish statistical significance.

04

C+ confusion matrix at the calibration cutoff

False matches and missed matches

At the calibration cutoff 0.3947772259513537, C+ classified 11 nonmatches as matches and missed 25 actual matches. These counts give precision 0.9375 and recall 0.8684. They describe this pair pool. A score alone is insufficient evidence for an automatic catalog merge.

C+ differences from L42 and C0

Relative to L42 on this pool, C+ AP was 1.63 percentage points higher and F1 was 4.96 percentage points higher. Relative to C0, AP was only 0.0381 percentage points higher. C0 is an independently trained control: all 42 feature values are zero during training and inference. It uses the six-epoch scheduler for four training epochs and is compared at epoch 4. The aggregate results do not establish a substantial feature-driven AP gain or statistical significance.

05

Metric definitions and cutoff dependence

AP

Average precision (AP) summarizes precision over the ranking of positive pairs. It uses the continuous pair scores and groups tied scores. AP 0.9660 is not a classification accuracy of 0.9660.

F1

F1 combines precision and recall after turning scores into match/nonmatch predictions at a cutoff. The cutoff is selected on calibration labels and fixed before evaluation. Changing it changes the balance of false matches and missed matches.

P@100

P@100 is the fraction of actual matches among the first 100 ranked pairs. Score ties at the boundary are ordered by pair_id. P@100 1.00 describes those 100 pairs in this pool, rather than all products or future listings.

06

Evaluation scope and unvalidated claims

The 555-pair pool is retrospective, and its history of use is not fully documented. No independent new Walmart–Amazon sample or entity-disjoint evaluation is established here. The saved aggregates provide no confidence interval or production latency/business-benefit measurement. Agreement in existing-weight replay verifies artifact preparation and prediction through the public code entry; it does not provide new unseen-data evidence.
